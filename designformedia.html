Design for Media

September 2025 - December 2025

Industry: Digital Design

Tools: Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop

Overview

My work in Design for Media exhibits my understanding of color theory and the effect of using color in design.

Projects

In 'Low Poly Poster' I captured an image of myself copying the lighting from a cinematic scene of my choice. I then posterized the image on Adobe Illustrator, and using complementary colors I created a dimensional low poly render of myself by individually creating triangle shapes that matched the shade of the image in black and white to the color I chose.

In 'Simultaneous Contrast' I created two different designs on Adobe Photoshop that achieved the visual illusion of one color appearing to be different on two different backgrounds (Example 1) and two colors looking the same on different colored backgrounds (Example 2).

Challenge

My most challenging project was the 'Composite Sculpture.' The objective was to create a pattern using household objects and overlay it onto a surface, building, or sculpture using the skills I learned in Adobe Photoshop.

I began by looking for interesting complementary colors in the real world. 'Leaf' caught my eye due to the vibrant green hue and interesting lines. 'Cloth' had the perfect contrast, along with a subtle but aesthetic pattern.

I used an image I found online, 'Pattern,' and the clipping mask tool to overlay 'Cloth' onto the unchanged 'Leaf.' That created 'Unit.'

I then reflected 'Unit' and repeated it until 'Motif,' the repetitive pattern, was created.

I took a picture of a sculpture I found interesting while out on a walk, and was able to overlay the pattern I made onto the sculpture. The most difficult part was warping, shading, and editing the vibrancy of the pattern to make sure that it looked natural on the sculpture.
