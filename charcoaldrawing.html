Charcoal Drawing

February 2026 - March 2026

Industry: Fine Arts

Tools: Charcoal

Overview

After understanding the basics of shapes and forms in two point perspective, I began drawing with charcoal. The '3 Dimensional Form' was the first drawing I made, learning how the basics of creating with charcoal using only three values.

Challenge

'Paper, Box and Reflective Spheres' and 'Bust' focused on shadows and reflections using a very short range of values. The goal was to only use a base shade, a darker shade, and white.

It was a challenge to capture the mirrored effect of the balls, the slight reflection of the light off of the table onto the box and bust, and the shadows on the paper and wall.

Final Project

For my final project, 'City Skyline' I was able to use a wider range of values and was supposed to focus on the diminution in the skyline. I am very proud of that drawing because I think it shows how much I grew in this class, and it was also featured in an art exhibition at my university.
