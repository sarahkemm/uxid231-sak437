Industry: Fine Arts

Tools: Graphite

Overview

In my introductory drawing class we focused on three dimensional shapes, two-point perspectives, and knowledge of forms through transparency. I gained an in-depth understanding of angles, scale, and how to get information from different objects and their relationships to create an entire picture.

Challenge

As a challenge I created this form called 'Cube Monster.' It started out as one single cube, and I carved out either rectangular depressions or obtrusions. It was difficult for me to keep it visually interesting while also keeping it technically accurate based on two-point perspective.

Process

Moving from simple boxes with straight lines and simple angles, I used my experience with squares and rectangles to draw more complicated shapes like spheres and cones. Even the 'Plastic Chair' started as a cube, and the angle of the legs and seat were added from there.

Unlike those simple shapes, the organic leaves of 'Plant and Box' were much more difficult to capture. Each leaf was created in relation to the details around it, similarly to the boxes. However, it required me to rely on the skills I had grown throughout the class to trust my own instincts on how shapes exist in three dimensional space.
