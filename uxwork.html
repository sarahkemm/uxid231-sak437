UX Research & Design

September 2025 to June 2026

Industry: User Experience & Interaction Design

Tools: Figma

Research

Overview

GoggleWorks is a nonprofit arts center offering art classes, a film theater, exhibitions, and a community for creatives of all levels.

Over a ten week period of focused UX research I aimed to increase engagement to reach more people in the community, and continue providing a space for creativity and support.

Key Areas

Based on the demographic of the area, I found that the most effective ways of outreach would be through:

  • In-person events
  • Flyers
  • Website visits

Methodology

I chose to conduct interviews with stakeholders and people who have been to GoggleWorks.

I then created user personas, an empathy map, and a user journey map.

Users

I asked about channels and promotion, common methods used with similar organizations, motivators. If they've been to GoggleWorks, their experience.

Wanted to assess how information usually spreads in order to lean into those methods.

Key Findings

Motivators:

  • Classes/learning something new are main motivators for coming to GoggleWorks
  • Fun events for parents to take their kids to
  • Catering to families will grow the community outreach: parents will tell other parents, parents will enroll their kids in classes if they have a good experience

Barriers:

  • Location and time is a huge barrier for these users
  • Huge reliance on word of mouth
  • Confusing for some users to use the website and events page

Opportunities & Recommendations

Events

  • Provide events that cater to different ages, and consider age when creating schedules
  • Promote future events and spread awareness of other services
  • More free events, but encourage donations.

Online Presence

  • Simple, clear instructions
  • Easier Navigation for when you arrive on-site
  • Make attractive events first and easy to spot

Low Fidelity

Overview

I created a low fidelity iteration of the events page, making a calendar so it is easier for people to see it in a visual format.

Goals

While designing I focused on specific tasks that users could complete during testing

  • Navigate to “Events” page
  • Go to/find the event on February 21st

Testing

Did three user tests where they completed the goals that I focused on designing.

Based on the feedback I received during the user interviews, I decided to make changes to the wireframe layout and focus on making components functional in the high fidelity iteration.

High Fidelity

Style Sheet

For the high fidelity wireframe of the events page created a comprehensive style sheet of fonts, type size, and color scheme. I included button, logo, and header components for the iPhone, iPad, and Macbook screens.

Components & Icons

While designing the pages, I created a library of components. This included a menu for both the iPad and iPhone, buttons for the event filter, and navigation. I also created a filter that could sort between free events, ticketed events, special events, and classes.

Calendar

My main focus and goal for the redesign of the Events page was a calendar that would be a visual aid to people and make navigation on the website easier. I used components and properties to create calendar cells that had an option for each screen size, have zero to one or more events, and show text or a dot as the signifier. The component had functional navigation that would take the user to the event or date.

Testing

After I completed my high fidelity design I carried out another round of user testing. While the project was done and I wouldn't be able to implement the changes they suggested, it gave me great insight on improvements I could make on future designs.

Takeaways

Through this GoggleWorks case study I gained familiarity with Figma, understanding of the user experience research and design process, and awareness of my own strengths and weaknesses within UX and UI.

As this was my first time using Figma, it gave me an introduction on how to use the tools, create components, and design pages that are aesthetically pleasing and functional.

Completing this project all the way from research to high fidelity design made me aware of the importance of staying consistent throughout the entire process. If this was a team project communication, respect, and collaboration would be crucial.
